# Excalidraw Plus: Professional AI Teaching & Presentation Studio

A full-fledged, modern Excalidraw whiteboard suite tailored for **teaching in professional sectors**, engineering, academia, and technical presentations.

---

## 🌟 Key Features (Matching Excalidraw Plus)

### 1. 🤖 AI Assistant (Text-to-Diagram & AI Slide Decks)
- **API Integrated**: Configured to work seamlessly with custom LLM API keys via in-app Settings or `.env`.
- **Single Canvas Diagrams**: Type any concept (e.g., *"Engineering Design Cycle"*, *"Cloud Architecture"*, *"Convolutional Neural Networks"*) and the AI generates structured visual diagrams with nodes, arrows, and colors.
- **4-Slide Teaching Deck Generator**: Automatically creates an end-to-end 4-slide curriculum deck (Objectives, Technical Architecture, Best Practices, and Summary Q&A) complete with teacher lecture notes!

### 2. 🎞️ Unlimited Scenes & Slide Deck Management
- Add, duplicate, delete, and rename slides/scenes on the fly.
- Switch between slides instantly while sketching during a live lecture.
- Automatically persists your entire project to browser local storage.

### 3. 📽️ Fullscreen Presentation Mode (PPT Slideshow)
- **Interactive Slideshow**: Fullscreen dark-stage mode for lecture halls or screen sharing.
- **🔴 Laser Pointer Tool**: Press `L` or toggle the button to project a smooth, glowing laser pointer onto the whiteboard to focus students' attention.
- **📝 Speaker & Lecture Notes**: Press `N` to display talking points and teaching cues.
- **Celebration Confetti**: Triggers celebratory confetti upon reaching the final summary slide.

### 4. 📊 PowerPoint (.PPTX) & PDF Export
- **Export to PowerPoint (`.pptx`)**: Generates real Microsoft PowerPoint widescreen (16:9) presentations with title slides, crisp whiteboard snapshots, and speaker notes attached.
- **Export to PDF**: Single-click PDF presentation generation for handouts.
- **Project Bundles**: Download and open full multi-slide `.json` presentation bundles.

### 5. 🏷️ Teaching Stencils & Annotations
- One-click stamp insertion for quick teaching markers:
  - `⭐ KEY PRINCIPLE`
  - `⚠️ EXAM / RISK POINT`
  - `✅ VERIFIED / APPROVED`
  - `💡 CLASS DISCUSSION`
