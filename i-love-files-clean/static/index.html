85A Deadleg Run & End Terminations (Chevron IS 85 / DL Guide)

Assigned to deadleg branch runs near the live process header and at deadleg terminations immediately upstream of end caps, blind flanges, or normally-closed block valves. Stagnant fluids drop out of suspension, leading to water accumulation and severe localized pitting corrosion.

85B Vertical Deadleg Low Base (Chevron IS 85)

Assigned to the base of vertical rises and gravity-settling pockets where gravity causes stagnant liquid phase separation and moisture drops to the lowest point of the piping loop.

51A Microbiologically Influenced Corrosion (Chevron IS 51)

Assigned to low-point drain terminals and stagnant deadleg dead-ends where absence of flow enables anaerobic biofilm colonization by sulfate-reducing bacteria (SRB), producing aggressive localized under-deposit pitting.

51B MIC Secondary Vertical Rise (Chevron IS 51)

Assigned to secondary branch risers subject to intermittent stagnant pooling and biological accumulation.

99 Foul Water Wet H2S Corrosion (Chevron IS 99)

Assigned to sour and foul water streams at directional flow disruptions (elbows, tees) where turbulent shear stress impinges on the pipe wall and prevents protective iron sulfide scale formation.

Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guides and answers to common questions about file conversions, security, and AutoCAD batch plotting.

How do I convert an Excel spreadsheet directly to PDF?
Click the Excel to PDF card in the File Converter tab. Drop your .xlsx or .xls workbook, and click Convert to PDF. The table headers, borders, and rows are automatically rendered into a crisp vector PDF.
How does AutoCAD Batch Plotting work?
Switch to the AutoCAD Batch Plot tab. Select your plotter, paper size (ANSI/ISO), and plot style (e.g. Circuitizer.ctb or monochrome.ctb). Drop 1 to 50+ DWG drawings and click Execute AutoCAD Batch Plot. The installed AutoCAD 2026 Core Console plots them with true lineweights, all layers turned on, and zooms to extents.
Are my files private and secure?
Yes. 100% of conversions, batch plots, and merges run locally on your system. No files are ever sent to third-party cloud servers.
How can I merge multiple documents and drawings into one PDF?
Switch to the Merge Files tab. Drop PDF specifications, Word documents (.docx), and architectural images. You can reorder the files in the queue and merge them into a single PDF portfolio.
How do I protect or redact a PDF?
In the File Converter tab, select Protect PDF to lock the file with AES-256 encryption. Or choose Redact PDF and enter sensitive keywords to permanently black them out and purge them from the document.
Who designed, engineered and built I LOVE FILES?
I LOVE FILES is designed, engineered, and maintained by Musthaque Ali (© 2026 Musthaque Ali. All Rights Reserved). Built with native AutoCAD Core Console plotting, resilient 3-tier Office/PDF engines, and zero cloud data leakage.
How does the 100% Client-Side WebAssembly Vector Engine work?
Our WebAssembly (WASM SIMD) pipeline compiles high-performance vector rendering and document manipulation libraries directly to run inside your web browser sandbox. Conversions like PDF page extraction, splitting, rotation, rasterization, and compression run at near-native CPU speeds (up to 420 MB/s) entirely on your device with 0 KB cloud network egress.
What are CTB Pen Tables and why do AutoCAD drawings need them?
Color-Dependent Plot Style Tables (.ctb) assign physical pen widths, line screening, and vector ink colors to AutoCAD's 255 ACI color indexes. Without a CTB table, drawings plot with faint hair-thin colors or illegible yellow text. ILOVEFILES supports standard plot tables (monochrome.ctb, acad.ctb, grayscale.ctb) and custom pen tables so your prints always match official architectural drawing standards.
What is the maximum file size limit for AutoCAD drawings and PDFs?
Free users can upload single or batch files up to 100 MB each with 5 free daily tasks. PRO and Enterprise accounts enjoy generous file allowances up to 2 GB per file, unlimited daily operations, and priority AutoCAD Core Console worker threads.
What cookies does ILOVEFILES use?
We exclusively use essential cookies and browser LocalStorage required for system functionality: remembering your Light/Dark theme preference, preserving your cookie consent status, and maintaining secure login sessions. We do not use third-party marketing or cross-site tracking cookies.
Can I integrate ILOVEFILES conversions into my backend or CI/CD?
Yes! Click Developer API Key in your account dropdown or footer to generate your secret API key. Our REST API supports simple cURL, Python, and Node.js requests to programmatically batch plot DWG/DXF drawings, convert Office docs, and compress PDF portfolios.
